### Privacy Policy
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.
### Who We Are
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning provides HVAC service, repair, installation, and maintenance in Hoboken, Jersey City, Hudson County, and nearby New Jersey service areas.
Business contact: 4 14th St #501, Hoboken, NJ 07030. Phone: 201-245-5151. Email: info@tanousnj.com.
### Information We Collect
When you submit a form, call us, or email us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, service request details, and any notes you choose to send.
Our website may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited, device information, IP address, form submission metadata, cookies, and security logs.
### How We Use Information
We use information to respond to service requests, schedule appointments, provide estimates, communicate with customers, maintain records, protect the website, and improve our marketing and website experience.
### Sharing Information
We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the website, process forms, send email, host data, secure the site, analyze traffic, or provide customer support.
### Cookies, Analytics, and Embedded Services
The website may use cookies and similar technologies for security, analytics, performance, forms, maps, video, fonts, or embedded third-party services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.
### How Long We Keep Information
We keep submitted information as long as needed for service, business records, customer communication, security, and legal or operational requirements.

To ask about this privacy policy or request changes to information you submitted, contact us at info@tanousnj.com or call 201-245-5151.
