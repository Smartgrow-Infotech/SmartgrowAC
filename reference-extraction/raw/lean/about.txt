### Founded in 2001. Still here.
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning started as a family-run HVAC shop in Hoboken in 2001. Same family, same trucks, same standard for 24+ years. We’ve grown through word-of-mouth in the Hudson County brownstone, condo, and commercial-building world — not through chasing the lowest bids.
When you call us, you reach a real person. When we quote a job, the price is the price. When we say we’ll be there, we are. ### What we stand for.
Honest answers. No fake "60-minute response" guarantees. No upselling you on a system you don’t need. If a $200 repair will get you another 5 years, that’s what we’ll recommend — not a $15,000 replacement.
Hudson County roots. We know which brownstones have asbestos ductwork. We know which condo buildings standardize on Friedrich PTAC. We know which Jersey City basements need tankless because there’s no room for a tank.
Real work. We take the rooftop crane jobs, the permit-required street closures, the condo-board negotiations — the work other contractors pass on. ### Why crane and permit work matters.
Replacing a rooftop HVAC unit in a Hoboken brownstone block isn’t a one-truck job. It needs a crane, city permits, sidewalk closure approvals, and police escorts for the lift. Most contractors won’t touch it.
We’ve done it dozens of times. You pay the city’s direct permit fees — never a markup from us. This is the work that keeps Hudson County condo associations and property managers coming back.
