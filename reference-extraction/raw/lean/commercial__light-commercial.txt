What we do
### HVAC for the in-between commercial spaces.
Most commercial HVAC vendors specialize at one extreme or the other: residential split systems on one end, massive corporate HVAC on the other. Light commercial — the offices, retail spaces, and small-business buildings that make up most of Hoboken/JC’s commercial real estate — falls in the gap.
We work it. Rooftop units on retail buildings, mini-splits in renovated storefronts, central HVAC in mid-size offices, dedicated server-room cooling. After-hours service so your business stays open. Predictable per-visit pricing, no surprise charges. Why light commercial works with us

What affects pricing
Light commercial HVAC pricing depends on the same factors as any commercial work, sized to your scale.
01
### Equipment type & size
Rooftop unit work has different costs than ductless or central systems. We’ll quote based on what’s actually installed — not generic per-sqft estimates.
02
### Work-hour window
Daytime work: standard rates. After-hours / weekend: modest premium, quoted up front. Most clients find after-hours saves more in lost business hours than the premium costs.
03
### Maintenance vs. ad-hoc
Annual contracts get better per-visit pricing and priority response. Some businesses prefer ad-hoc — we’re fine with both.

### Common questions from light commercial clients.
Do you do single-tenant office buildings? Yes — offices from 1,000 sqft up to 30,000 sqft are squarely in our wheelhouse. Single-tenant or multi-tenant, owner-occupied or leased. We handle the work, you handle the business.
What about server-room or IDF cooling? Yes — small-office server rooms typically need dedicated cooling (a mini-split or small rooftop unit). We size and install these to handle the 24/7 load that office HVAC isn’t designed for.
Medical or dental offices? Yes — medical/dental offices have specific HVAC requirements (filtration, isolation rooms in some cases, dedicated equipment cooling). We work within your existing infection-control protocols and HIPAA expectations.
How fast can you respond to a no-cool emergency in summer? Same-day on most calls if you reach us in the morning. Maintenance contract clients get priority dispatch. After-hours emergency response available at modest premium.
Can you handle a building with mixed uses (retail downstairs, residential upstairs)? Yes — mixed-use is common in Hoboken. We handle the commercial HVAC and can also handle residential systems above. Single point of contact for the whole building.
Do I need a long-term contract for repair work? No — we do plenty of one-off repair work on a T&M basis. Many clients eventually move to a maintenance contract once they see the value, but there’s no pressure either way.
