Why annual maintenance
### What yearly AC tune-ups actually do for you.
Maintenance isn’t a polite formality — it’s the cheapest way to keep cooling reliable through Hudson County’s humid summers. ### Keeps your warranty valid
Almost every manufacturer (Fujitsu, Carrier, Trane, etc.) requires documented annual service to honor the parts warranty. Skip it and you’re on your own when a compressor fails. ### Catches refrigerant leaks early
A slow leak quietly kills efficiency and damages the compressor. Catching it during a tune-up is a $200 fix; catching it after the compressor fails is $2,500+. ### Prevents emergency calls
Most July emergency calls trace back to issues that were visible the previous spring — dirty coils, weak capacitors, loose wiring. We catch them before they break. ### Restores 15–30% efficiency
Dirty coils, clogged filters, and low refrigerant force the system to work harder. A tune-up usually restores measurable efficiency — visible in your utility bill. ### Extends system life
Well-maintained AC commonly lasts 15–18 years. Neglected systems often fail by year 10. Maintenance pays for itself many times over. ### Hygiene & air quality
Coil mold, drain biofilm, and filter dust all show up in your indoor air. A proper clean leaves the system fresh going into peak season.

### Common questions about AC maintenance.
What’s actually included in a tune-up? Refrigerant level check, coil cleaning (evaporator + condenser), drain line clear, electrical connection tightening, capacitor & contactor test, thermostat calibration, blower motor & filter check, airflow measurement, and a written findings report. About 60–90 minutes per system.
Single visit vs. Combo Plan — which is right? If you have AC and heating, the Combo Plan ($245) is almost always the better deal — covers both seasons and beats two single visits ($185 + $185 = $370). Single $185 makes sense if you only have one season’s system.
When should I schedule? Spring AC tune-ups go best in April–May, before peak demand. Once it’s 85°F+ in Hoboken, the schedule fills fast. Booking ahead also means you pick the time, not us.
My building has 4 units. Can you do them all at once? Yes — we handle multi-unit condo and rental buildings every week. We quote per-unit, schedule in one visit window, and provide a single invoice or per-unit reports for property managers.
Can I skip a year? You can — but the warranty clock keeps running. Most manufacturer warranties require documented annual service to honor parts coverage. Skip a year and a $1,200 compressor claim could get denied.
Does maintenance cover ductless mini-splits? Yes. Ductless heads have washable filters, indoor coils, and condensate lines that need annual attention. We service Fujitsu, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Daikin, and others.
