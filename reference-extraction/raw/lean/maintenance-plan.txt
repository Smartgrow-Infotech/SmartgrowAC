Quick answers
### Common questions about the maintenance plan.
What if my system breaks down between visits? Maintenance plan members get priority scheduling on repair calls + free diagnostic visits + 15% off any repair work. Repairs are still billed separately, but at member pricing.
Can I cancel anytime? Yes — no contract, no cancellation fee. We bill annually; cancel any time and we won't charge for the next year.
What about multi-system homes (e.g., AC + boiler + 2 zones)? Custom pricing — usually $245 + ~$75 per additional system. Call us for a quote based on your actual setup.
Do you do PTAC service contracts for condo buildings? Yes, but those are quoted custom based on # of units, building access, and visit frequency. Typical condo PTAC contracts run $40-80 per unit per year. Commercial contracts page →
When do you schedule the visits? Spring AC tune-up: March-May. Fall heating tune-up: September-November. We email/text to schedule a few weeks ahead — pick the timeslot that works for you.
What if I'm already due for a tune-up when I sign up? We'll schedule your first visit right away if needed. Your annual cycle resets from that visit.
