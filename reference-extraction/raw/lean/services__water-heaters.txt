### Common water heater problems.
If you’re dealing with any of these in your Hudson County home, we’ve seen it hundreds of times. ### No hot water at all
Pilot light out, tripped breaker, blown heating element, gas valve failure, or control board issue. We diagnose before any work. ### Running out of hot water fast
Undersized tank for your household, sediment buildup reducing capacity, broken dip tube, or thermostat issue. Often a same-day fix. ### Leaking around the base
Often means the tank itself has corroded through — usually time to replace. Sometimes a loose drain valve or T&P valve, which we can fix. ### Rumbling or popping noises
Sediment buildup at the tank bottom, especially with NJ hard water. We flush the tank and check anode rod — extends life significantly. ### Discolored or rusty water
Anode rod has corroded away, leaving tank metal exposed. Replace the rod first; if water stays rusty, the tank itself is failing. ### Pilot light won't stay lit
Bad thermocouple, draft issues, clogged pilot orifice, or gas valve problem. Common on older gas water heaters — usually a quick repair.

### Common questions about water heaters.
How long do water heaters last? Tank water heaters: typically 8-12 years. Tankless: 15-20 years with annual maintenance. Combi boilers: 15-20 years. NJ hard water shortens tank life if you skip annual flushing.
Tank vs tankless — which is right for me? Tankless wins on: endless hot water, basement space saved, longer lifespan, lower energy bills. Tank wins on: lower upfront cost, simpler install, works during power outages (gas-only models). For Hoboken brownstones with tight basements, tankless is often the right call. We’ll size and recommend based on your household.
Can a tankless water heater work in a Hoboken brownstone? Yes — tankless is actually ideal for older Hoboken/JC buildings. Mounts on the wall (frees up basement space), runs on gas or electric, and the right-sized unit handles 2-3 simultaneous fixtures. Main consideration: gas line capacity and venting path. We handle both during install.
How often should I drain my water heater? Once a year minimum for tank units. NJ hard water deposits sediment fast — annual flushing extends tank life by 3-5 years and keeps efficiency up. Tankless: descale once every 1-2 years depending on water hardness. Our Maintenance Plan ($245/yr) includes this.
Should I switch from electric to gas water heater? Usually yes if your home already has gas service. Gas water heaters cost less to operate (NJ gas is cheaper per BTU than electricity), heat water faster, and last longer. Conversion involves running a gas line, venting, and a switch in fuel source — we handle it all.
What size water heater do I need? Tank: 40-gallon for 1-2 people, 50-gal for 3-4, 75-gal for 5+. Tankless: sized by GPM (gallons per minute) based on simultaneous fixtures. A 4-person Hudson County household typically needs a 7-9 GPM tankless. We do a proper sizing calc, not a guess.
