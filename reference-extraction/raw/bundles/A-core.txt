### Emergency HVAC across Hudson County.
Phones answered around the clock. We’ll tell you straight what to expect for on-site arrival based on the situation, your location, and current demand — no fake "60-minute" promises.
 {LINK:tel:2012455151} Call 201-245-5151 now
When to call us right away
### Common HVAC emergencies.
If you’re dealing with any of these, don’t wait — call us. Faster response = less damage. ### No heat in winter
Freezing temps + dead furnace/boiler = pipe-freezing risk within hours. Call us immediately to avoid much bigger water damage. ### AC out in summer heat wave
Especially with elderly, infants, or pets in the home. Indoor temps in NJ summers reach dangerous levels fast. ### Water heater leaking
Active water leaking from the base of a tank means the tank itself is failing. Shut off the water + power, then call us. ### Gas smell near furnace/boiler
Leave the home immediately and call PSE&G’s emergency line first (800-880-7734). Then call us once it’s safe. ### CO detector going off
Leave the home, call 911, then call us. Carbon monoxide from a furnace problem is life-threatening — don’t wait. ### Building HVAC system failure
Property managers + condo boards: rooftop or central plant failures during occupancy. We move fast on commercial contracts.
While we’re on the way
### What to do while waiting.
Small things that can prevent bigger problems before we arrive.
### No heat in winter
Open cabinet doors under sinks (lets warm air reach pipes). Let faucets drip slightly. Keep the home above 55°F by any means — portable heaters in occupied rooms only.
### Water leak
Shut off the water supply to the leaking unit. For water heaters: turn off the gas or breaker at the panel. Move valuables away from the area.
### AC out in summer
Close blinds and curtains. Use fans to circulate air. Move vulnerable family members (elderly, infants) to the coolest room. Stay hydrated.
Our promise
### No fake arrival-time guarantees. Just an honest answer.
Other contractors promise "60-minute response" or "same-day guaranteed." We don’t. Phones are answered 24/7 by real people who’ll tell you the truth about what to expect — based on the actual situation, the day’s schedule, and how far we have to travel.
When we say we’ll be there in 2 hours, we mean it. When we can’t get there until tomorrow morning, we tell you that too — so you can make a real decision.

Who NEIF is
### National Energy Improvement Fund — financing built for energy-efficiency upgrades.
01 — What NEIF covers
### Energy-efficient HVAC
Heat pumps, high-efficiency furnaces, tankless water heaters, ductless mini-splits, and whole-home IAQ systems. Pairs with NJ Clean Energy Program rebates.
02 — Who qualifies
### Residential homeowners
NJ residential homeowners with reasonable credit. NEIF works directly with you on application + terms — we just help connect you. Commercial customers should work through their own banks.
03 — Why financing
### Big upgrades, monthly payments
A $15K heat pump install becomes $250/month. Often pays for itself in energy savings + you don’t front the whole cost at once. Combine with NJ + federal rebates for maximum value.
How it works
### 3 steps to financed HVAC.
No pressure, no obligation. You can step away at any point. ### Free in-home estimate
We come out, look at your existing system, recommend the right upgrade, and quote the full project — no obligation. ### NEIF pre-qualification
If you want to finance, we connect you with NEIF. Their online pre-qualification is a soft credit check — no impact on your score. ### Install + monthly payments
We install the system. NEIF handles the financing. You make monthly payments directly to NEIF, not us.
Quick answers
### Common questions about financing.
Why NEIF specifically? NEIF specializes in energy-efficiency home upgrades. They understand HVAC pricing, work with NJ rebate programs, and have terms designed for big-ticket home improvements (not generic consumer loans).
What credit score do I need? NEIF works with a range of credit profiles. The best terms go to 680+ scores, but they have options for lower scores too. The soft-credit pre-qualification step tells you what you'd actually be approved for, no commitment.
What are typical interest rates? Rates vary by credit + loan amount, generally 6-15% APR. NEIF will give you exact numbers after pre-qualification. Often paired with NJ Clean Energy and federal IRA rebates that reduce your total cost.
How long are loan terms? Typically 5-15 years. Longer terms = lower monthly payment but more interest total. We can model both for you.
Is there a fee for using NEIF financing? No fee from us. NEIF may have small origination fees on the loan itself — they'll disclose all of that during pre-qualification.
Does commercial financing work the same way? No — commercial customers typically work with their own banks or PACE financing. NEIF is residential-focused. Commercial work →
