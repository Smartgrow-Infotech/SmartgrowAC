Do you service Fort Lee high-rises and single-family homes? Yes — both ends of Fort Lee’s housing spectrum. Linwood and the older single-family stock: central HVAC replacement, furnace work, ductless add-ons. Same approach, scaled to the property.
How fast can Tanous reach Fort Lee from Hoboken? 15–25 minutes from our 14th Street shop, depending on Boulevard East traffic and Fort Lee Road access. Do you have relationships with Fort Lee condo HOAs? Yes — we work with several of the major Fort Lee condo buildings on per-unit programs and common-area contracts.
