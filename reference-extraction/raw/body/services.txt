### Repair or replace?
Quick decision factors. Or just call us — we’ll give you an honest answer either way. ### Repair makes sense if…
- System is under 10 years old
- Repair cost is under 30% of a new system
- Recent annual maintenance, no major issues
- Single component failure (capacitor, ignitor, sensor)
{LINK:/services/}Schedule a repair → ### Replace makes sense if…
- System is 12+ years old
- Repair cost is 50%+ of replacement
- R-22 refrigerant (phased out by EPA)
- Energy bills rising despite use staying steady
