### Common questions about HVAC systems.
How do I know which HVAC system is right for my home? Depends on your building type, ductwork situation, and budget. Brownstones & row homes without ducts: ductless mini-splits. Modern condos: PTAC or water-source heat pump. Single-family homes: central HVAC or heat pumps. We do free in-home assessments and recommend honestly, even if it’s not the most expensive option.
Do you install systems you didn’t originally sell? Yes — we service every major brand whether or not we installed it. If your existing system breaks down, we’ll fix it. If it’s time to replace, we’ll recommend a fit for your space and budget.
What’s the difference between a heat pump and a ductless mini-split? Mini-splits ARE a type of heat pump — specifically, ductless heat pumps. Traditional heat pumps use ductwork (like central AC). Mini-splits use individual wall-mounted units. Both heat and cool. The choice depends on whether you have ductwork.
Why is PTAC so common in Hoboken condos? PTACs (Packaged Terminal Air Conditioners) install through an exterior wall — perfect for condo buildings where running ductwork between units isn’t practical. Friedrich is the market leader and what most Hudson County condo buildings standardize on.
Can I get NJ rebates on heat pump installs? Often yes — NJ Clean Energy Program and federal Inflation Reduction Act both offer heat pump rebates and tax credits. We’ll help you identify which ones apply to your install and provide the documentation you need.
Do you handle commercial HVAC systems? Yes — it’s our bread and butter. Rooftop units, water-source heat pumps for buildings, PTAC service contracts for condo associations. See our {LINK:/commercial/}commercial page for the full picture.
