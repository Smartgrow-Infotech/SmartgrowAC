What it is
### How whole-home air purifiers work.
A media cabinet (housing a thick MERV 11–16 filter) gets installed on your return-air ductwork — just before the air handler. All air circulating through your HVAC passes through it first. Captures dust, pollen, pet dander, mold spores, and combustion particles down to a few microns.
For viruses and bacteria, a UV-C light gets installed in the supply plenum — sterilizes microorganisms as they pass through. Combined, they handle the vast majority of indoor air contaminants that a 1" furnace filter won’t touch. Why air purifiers in Hudson County
### Urban air, old buildings, and seasonal allergies.
Hudson County indoor air carries unique stress — tunnel diesel particulates, river-valley pollen, and old-building dust. Whole-home purifiers handle what a 1" filter can’t. ### Captures sub-micron particulates
MERV 13+ filters catch pollen, pet dander, mold spores, and combustion soot down to 0.3 microns. Old 1" furnace filters catch maybe 25% of that — a real upgrade for anyone with allergies or asthma. ### Whole-home, not just one room
Portable HEPA air purifiers clean one room. Whole-home media filters clean every room that gets HVAC airflow. The math heavily favors whole-home for any house with 3+ rooms. ### Lower furnace strain
Counterintuitively, a bigger filter that catches more dust also reduces strain on the blower — because thick media filters have far more surface area than 1" filters, despite higher MERV ratings. Equipment lasts longer.

Sizing & options
### Picking the right air purifier setup.
Whole-home air purification is more configurable than people realize. Three or four decisions drive the final spec.
01
### Filter MERV rating
MERV 11: good general filtration, low pressure drop. MERV 13: catches most allergens, mild pressure increase. MERV 16: near-HEPA, best for severe allergies, requires blower verification. Higher isn’t always better — depends on your system.
02
### Media cabinet size
4" or 5" thick media cabinets are standard for whole-home installs. Larger cabinets = larger filter surface = longer change intervals (6–12 months) and lower pressure drop than thin filters.
03
### UV-C addition
UV-C light installed in the supply plenum sterilizes airborne viruses, bacteria, and mold growing on the coil. Lower priority than media filtration but valuable for homes with immune-compromised residents.
04
### Smart monitoring
Some systems include indoor air quality sensors (particulates, VOCs) that report to a thermostat or app. Useful if you want to verify the system is actually doing its job over time.
What installation involves
Air purifier installs are clean retrofits to existing HVAC systems. Three decisions drive scope.
01
### Existing ductwork access
Media cabinet needs ~4–6 inches of space upstream of the air handler on the return-air side. Most installs find space; a few require minor ductwork rework.
02
### Blower capacity
Higher-MERV filters create more pressure drop. We verify your blower can handle the increase — for most modern variable-speed systems, no problem. For older single-speed blowers, sometimes we recommend a thicker media cabinet to compensate.
03
### Electrical for UV-C
UV-C lights need a small 120V circuit. Usually pulled from the existing furnace circuit. Quick add-on.

### Common questions about air purifiers.
Is a whole-home purifier really better than a portable HEPA? For a single room with a closed door — portable HEPA wins. For everywhere else in the house, whole-home is dramatically better. Most allergy/asthma sufferers benefit far more from whole-home filtration because it covers every room the HVAC reaches.
What MERV rating should I get? MERV 13 is the sweet spot for most homes — catches pollen, pet dander, mold spores, smoke particulates, and PM2.5 from tunnel/highway traffic. MERV 16 is for severe allergies or immune-compromised households. MERV 11 is the lower-cost upgrade from a 1" filter.
How often do I change the filter? 4" media cabinet: every 6–12 months for most homes. 5" cabinet: every 12 months. Pet households or NYC-area particulate exposure: closer to the 6-month end. We’ll check at your tune-up if you’re on a plan.
Does UV-C actually do anything? Properly sized and placed UV-C kills airborne viruses, bacteria, and mold on the coil — verified in lab and field studies. The catch: it has to be positioned correctly with adequate dwell time. We install it right or not at all.
Can I upgrade my existing 1" filter to MERV 13 instead of installing a media cabinet? Possibly, but pressure drop on a 1" MERV 13 filter is significant — can strain older single-speed blowers and reduce airflow to your AC coil (leading to icing). A proper 4" media cabinet is the right way to upgrade filtration without the side effects.
Do I need an air purifier with my HVAC? Need is a strong word — the system runs fine without one. But if anyone in the home has allergies, asthma, or respiratory sensitivity, the difference is real. For pet households and homes near busy roads/tunnels, the case is even stronger.
