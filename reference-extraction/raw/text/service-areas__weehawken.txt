Weehawken HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Weehawken, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — about 5 minutes up the Hudson palisade. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for the full Weehawken housing range: Boulevard East high-rise condos, Hudson Heights row homes, Old Weehawken single-family, and Port Imperial waterfront condos. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we work the buildings most Weehawken HVAC contractors avoid — especially the high-rise PTAC programs on Boulevard East.
Why Weehawken works with us
### Weehawken’s housing splits clearly along the palisade.
Weehawken splits into roughly three housing zones, each with different HVAC realities. The Boulevard East high-rises (the iconic NYC-view buildings perched on the cliff) run on PTAC arrays and water-source heat pumps — we service both, including per-unit programs for the HOAs. Hudson Heights row homes are pre-war stock similar to old Hoboken — usually steam or hydronic heat and no central AC, requiring ductless retrofits. Port Imperial waterfront condos at the base of the palisade are newer construction with mixed HVAC strategies.
What unites the work: short response times. We’re 5 minutes away, we know the building stock, and we know which crane companies have permits to work on Boulevard East. Most Weehawken HVAC contractors decline the cliff-side high-rise work because of the access logistics. We don’t.

Frequently asked in Weehawken
### Common questions from Weehawken homeowners.
Do you service the Boulevard East high-rises in Weehawken?
 [svg] 
Yes — most local contractors avoid Boulevard East work because of the cliff-side access logistics. We service most of the major Boulevard East condo buildings on per-unit PTAC programs and common-area HVAC. We know which crane companies have the right permits for cliff-side lifts.
How fast can Tanous reach Weehawken from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
Most of Weehawken is 5–10 minutes from our 14th Street shop. Boulevard East cliff-top buildings sometimes add 5 minutes for parking and freight access logistics, but we plan around it. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 60 minutes.
Can you install ductless AC in a Hudson Heights row home?
 [svg] 
Yes — Hudson Heights row homes are essentially Hoboken-era brownstone stock and we work them the same way. Fujitsu ductless mini-splits, line sets routed through closets or behind moldings, condensers placed in rear yards or on roof. Most installs in 1–2 days.
Do you handle steam boilers in old Weehawken buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes. Many Hudson Heights and Old Weehawken homes still run on original steam systems — we service the boiler, radiators, and steam piping. Knowing which steam systems are worth repairing vs. converting to hydronic is part of what 25 years of local experience gets you.
Do you do work at Port Imperial?
 [svg] 
Yes — Port Imperial condos at the foot of the palisade are newer construction with mixed-system HVAC. We service per-unit PTAC, water-source heat pumps where used, and common-area systems. HOA coordination for unit work handled.
