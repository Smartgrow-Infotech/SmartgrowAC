Quick decision factors. Or just call us — we’ll give you an honest answer either way.

### Repair makes sense if…
- System is under 10 years old
- Repair cost is under 30% of a new system
- Recent annual maintenance, no major issues
- Single component failure (capacitor, ignitor, sensor)

- Repair cost is 50%+ of replacement

What we see most
### Common AC problems we fix.
If you’re experiencing any of these, we’ve seen it hundreds of times in Hudson County homes and buildings.
[svg] 
### AC won't turn on
Tripped breaker, blown capacitor, bad thermostat, or control board failure. We diagnose and quote before any work.
[svg] 
### Blowing warm air
Low refrigerant, frozen evaporator coil, dirty filter, or failed compressor. Often a same-day fix.
[svg] 
### Strange noises
Loose parts, worn bearings, refrigerant hiss, or ductwork issues. We’ll find the source.
[svg] 
### Leaking water
Clogged condensate drain, ice buildup on the coil, or a failed condensate pump. Quick diagnostic.
[svg] 
### Short cycling
Oversized unit, bad thermostat placement, or low refrigerant. Often points to install issues.
[svg] 
### High utility bills
Dirty coils, leaky ducts, aging compressor, or wrong-size system. We’ll show you the math.

Quick answers
### Common questions about AC service.
How often should I service my AC?
 [svg] 
Once a year — ideally in spring before the cooling season starts. Annual maintenance catches refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and electrical issues early, before they become emergency repair calls. Our Combo Plan ($245/yr) covers both spring AC + fall heating tune-ups.
How do I know if I need a new AC system?
 [svg] 
Common signals: system is 12+ years old, repair cost is 50%+ of replacement, R-22 refrigerant (phased out by EPA), or utility bills climbing despite usage staying the same. We’ll give you an honest comparison — sometimes a repair is the right call even on older systems.
What size AC do I need for my home?
 [svg] 
Sizing depends on square footage, insulation, ceiling height, window count, sun exposure, and ductwork (if any). We do a Manual J load calculation for every install — not a quick eyeball estimate. Right-sizing saves you 15-30% on operating cost and keeps comfort even.
Do you install ductless AC for brownstones without ductwork?
 [svg] 
Yes — this is one of our strongest specialties. Ductless mini-splits (Fujitsu, primarily) are ideal for Hoboken brownstones, Jersey City row homes, and renovated condos where running ductwork through plaster walls isn’t practical or affordable. Single-zone systems run $4,000-$7,000 installed; multi-zone $10,000-$20,000+.
How long does an AC install take?
 [svg] 
Most ductless mini-split installs: 1-2 days. Central AC replacement (existing ductwork): 1 day. Full new central AC system (new ductwork): 2-4 days. We’ll walk you through the timeline before scheduling.
Do you offer emergency AC repair?
 [svg] 
Yes. Phones answered 24/7 across Hudson County. We’ll tell you what to expect for on-site arrival based on the situation, your location, and current demand — no fake "60-minute guarantees."
