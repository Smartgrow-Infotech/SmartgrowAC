Cliffside Park HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Cliffside Park, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — about 15 minutes north along the Palisades. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for Cliffside Park’s mostly-residential housing: single-family along Anderson Avenue, multi-family along Palisade Avenue, and the condos near the Palisades edge. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we cover the residential HVAC realities that define Cliffside Park property.
Why Cliffside Park works with us
### Cliffside Park sits on the Palisades — residential character runs along the cliff.
Cliffside Park has a quieter residential character than its waterfront neighbors. Single-family homes along Anderson Avenue, multi-family along Palisade Avenue, and condos near the cliff edge make up most of the housing. Central HVAC is the dominant system — we do plenty of furnace replacements, AC swap-outs, and ductwork work along these streets. The Anderson Avenue corridor has consistent suburban-style residential rhythms that we’ve worked for 25 years.
Some Cliffside Park stock is older single-family that originally ran on oil or non-condensing gas furnaces — ripe for replacement with modern condensing equipment. The Palisade Avenue multi-family is a different rhythm — small-landlord properties with shared boilers and per-unit cooling decisions. We handle both. NEIF financing on residential replacements applies just like elsewhere in our service area.

Frequently asked in Cliffside Park
### Common questions from Cliffside Park homeowners.
Do you service Cliffside Park homes along the Palisades?
 [svg] 
Yes — single-family along Anderson Avenue and the streets near the Palisades edge are regular work for us. Central HVAC replacement, furnace swaps, AC repair, ductless retrofits for additions. About 15 minutes from our Hoboken shop.
How fast can Tanous reach Cliffside Park?
 [svg] 
15–20 minutes from our 14th Street Hoboken shop, depending on Boulevard East and River Road traffic. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 75–90 minutes. We work plenty of homes here, so dispatch is familiar with the addresses.
Do you handle oil-to-gas conversions in older Cliffside Park homes?
 [svg] 
Yes — some older Cliffside Park homes still run oil-burning furnaces or boilers. We do oil-to-gas conversions: new gas service line coordination, high-efficiency gas furnace install, removal of oil tank, etc. NJ has incentives for these conversions when they make sense.
Can you do central HVAC replacement in a Cliffside Park single-family home?
 [svg] 
Yes — bread-and-butter work for us. Most Cliffside Park single-family has existing ductwork in serviceable shape. Replacement AC + furnace as a matched pair is typically 1–2 days. Manual J load calc included on every install.
Do you do work in the Palisades-edge condo buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes — mid-rise condos near the cliff edge typically use either PTAC or central systems depending on building age. We service per-unit equipment and common-area HVAC for both.
