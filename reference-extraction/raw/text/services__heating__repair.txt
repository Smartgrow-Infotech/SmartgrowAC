Symptoms we diagnose
### What’s your heat doing — or not doing?
We’ve seen all of these hundreds of times across Hudson County brownstones, condos, and old-Hoboken boiler rooms. Most have a clear diagnostic path.
[svg] 
### No heat at all
Pilot/ignition failure, blown control board, dead thermostat, or low fuel pressure. We isolate the cause before quoting parts.
[svg] 
### Uneven heat between rooms
Ductwork leaks, undersized returns, failed zone valves, or a thermostat in the wrong spot. Often fixable without replacing equipment.
[svg] 
### Strange smells
Gas, exhaust, or burning dust — each means something different. Gas smell is leave-the-house-and-call us. The others usually aren’t emergencies.
[svg] 
### Short cycling
Furnace turns on and off in minutes. Usually overheating from a dirty filter, bad limit switch, or oversized unit. Often a same-day fix.
[svg] 
### Banging or whistling
Steam radiators banging is almost always pitched piping or air-vent failure. Hydronic whistling is usually trapped air. We know the patterns.
[svg] 
### Pilot or ignition won’t light
Thermocouple, gas valve, igniter, or flame sensor. Standard diagnostic order, standard parts. Most jobs same-day.
How a repair visit works
### Diagnose first. Quote next. Then fix.
Heating systems range from a 1950s steam boiler to a 2024 heat pump — same diagnostic discipline applies to both.
01
### Phone triage
We ask what the system’s doing, what brand and age, and whether it’s a true emergency — before we send a truck. Saves you a trip fee on simple fixes.
02
### On-site diagnostic
Full system check — not just the loudest symptom. We isolate the actual cause so the repair fixes the real problem.
03
### Honest quote
Parts, labor, and your options in writing before any work starts. If repair vs. replace is close, we’ll show you both numbers.
04
### Fix & verify
We repair, test the full cycle, and explain what we did. Clean up. Most jobs get a workmanship warranty on top of the parts warranty.
What affects the cost
Heating repair pricing is even more varied than AC. The system itself, its age, and access all play in.
01
### System type
A failed gas valve on a modern condensing boiler is different work than a soot-clogged old steam boiler. Hydronic, steam, and forced-air all have different diagnostic paths.
02
### Parts & age
Some older boilers (especially pre-2000) use parts that have to be sourced specially. We’ll tell you on the phone whether it’s a stock part or a longer wait.
03
### Steam vs. hydronic complexity
Old-Hoboken steam systems often need pipe access, low-water cutoff testing, and skim work that hydronic doesn’t. Old buildings, more time.

Quick answers
### Common questions about heating repair.
When is “no heat” a true emergency?
 [svg] 
Below ~25°F outside, with pipes at risk of freezing, or vulnerable people (infants, elderly, medical needs) in the home — that’s an emergency call. Mild evening with extra blankets and a working fireplace — usually fine until morning. We’ll help you decide on the phone.
Boiler vs. furnace vs. heat pump — what’s the difference?
 [svg] 
Boilers heat water (steam or hot-water/hydronic) that warms radiators or radiant floors. Furnaces heat air and blow it through ducts. Heat pumps use refrigerant to move heat — same equipment as central AC, just reversed. Each has its own repair patterns. Hoboken/JC has all three in heavy use.
Do you service old steam radiators?
 [svg] 
Yes — this is one of the most common heating systems in old Hoboken brownstones. Steam boilers, radiator air vents, low-water cutoffs, sight-glass cleaning. We know what good and bad steam looks like.
Heat pumps in winter — do they work?
 [svg] 
Modern cold-climate heat pumps work down to about 5°F with full output. Below that, most installs have a backup (gas furnace or electric strip). If yours seems weak in cold weather, it could be a refrigerant or defrost issue worth diagnosing.
Is my 25-year-old boiler worth repairing?
 [svg] 
Maybe. If it’s a cast-iron boiler with sound piping, you can sometimes get another decade out of it for a few hundred dollars. If the heat exchanger is cracked or you’re burning oil at $4/gallon, replacement starts to look better. We’ll give you both numbers.
Warranty?
 [svg] 
Parts: manufacturer warranty (typically 1–10 years for major components depending on brand). Labor: our workmanship warranty on the repair. We document everything on the invoice.
