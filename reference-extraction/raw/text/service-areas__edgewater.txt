Edgewater HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Edgewater, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 10–15 minutes north along the Hudson River. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for Edgewater’s waterfront housing: River Road condo high-rises, Edgewater Place developments, City Place mixed-use, and the townhomes along the Russell Avenue corridor. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re built for the Hudson River waterfront condo realities that define modern Edgewater property.
Why Edgewater works with us
### Edgewater is condo country — PTAC and WSHP work dominates.
Edgewater’s housing is mostly newer construction along the Hudson River waterfront — River Road condos, Edgewater Place, and the modern mixed-use developments at City Place. The HVAC reality reflects this: most units use PTAC arrays (Friedrich-spec in many buildings) or water-source heat pump systems (in the more modern construction). Per-unit replacement programs and HOA contracts are our standard mode of working here.
We work most of the major Edgewater condo buildings on per-unit programs. The high-rise locations along River Road have specific access logistics (freight elevator booking, after-hours requirements) that we’ve standardized on. The newer Edgewater Place and City Place buildings use mixed PTAC + WSHP, and we service both. Building management companies and HOA boards typically prefer us because the per-unit pricing is predictable and the COI process is well-established.

Frequently asked in Edgewater
### Common questions from Edgewater HOA boards & residents.
Do you service Edgewater high-rise PTAC systems?
 [svg] 
Yes — PTAC replacement and service in Edgewater high-rises is a major part of our business. River Road condos use Friedrich PTACs almost across the board, and we run multi-year rolling replacement programs for several of the major buildings. HOA-friendly contracts, per-unit invoicing, freight elevator coordination handled.
How fast can Tanous reach Edgewater from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
10–15 minutes from our 14th Street shop, depending on River Road traffic. High-rise condo access adds 5–10 minutes for freight elevator and building check-in logistics. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 75–90 minutes.
Do you do water-source heat pump work in Edgewater?
 [svg] 
Yes — the newer Edgewater Place and City Place developments use water-source heat pumps in many units. We service per-unit WSHPs and coordinate with building engineers for loop-side work. Per-unit replacement is usually a half-day job.
Can you handle building-wide HVAC for Edgewater condos?
 [svg] 
Yes — both per-unit programs and common-area HVAC service (lobby, hallways, gym, business center). One contract covers everything mechanical in the building. COI, insurance, NJ licensing all current and on file with most major Edgewater management companies.
Do you do residential work in townhomes off River Road?
 [svg] 
Yes — Russell Avenue townhomes and the smaller residential developments off the main waterfront have central HVAC similar to North Bergen single-family work. Replacement, ductwork rework, heat pump conversions. Same approach as residential elsewhere in Hudson County.
