Jersey City HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Jersey City, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 5 minutes from Downtown JC. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for the full range of Jersey City housing: Downtown waterfront condos, Hamilton Park and Paulus Hook row homes, The Heights single-family and multi-family buildings, and Journal Square high-rises. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re built for Jersey City’s housing diversity — from 1880s brownstone retrofits to 2020s condo PTAC programs.
Why Jersey City works with us
### Jersey City’s diversity is what makes the work interesting.
Jersey City has more housing variety than any other Hudson County town — brownstones in Hamilton Park, glass towers Downtown, century-old row homes in The Heights, single-family in Greenville and Bergen-Lafayette. Each calls for different HVAC strategy. We’ve done all of them: Fujitsu ductless retrofits in Heights brownstones, PTAC programs in the Newport towers, central HVAC swaps in the post-war single-family stock along Kennedy Boulevard.
The other Jersey City reality: many homeowners are first-time buyers in century-old buildings, dealing with HVAC realities that surprise them — steam boilers in basements, old fuse panels, plaster walls that resist any kind of demo. We default to honest, plain-language explanation. No upselling, no scare tactics — just the right answer for the building you bought.

Frequently asked in Jersey City
### Common questions from Jersey City homeowners.
Do you service Jersey City Heights row homes and brownstones?
 [svg] 
Yes — The Heights is a meaningful chunk of our weekly work. Most Heights brownstones and row homes have steam or old hydronic heating plus no central AC. We retrofit Fujitsu ductless for cooling and service the existing heating — or replace it with modern condensing equipment if it’s time.
How fast can Tanous reach Jersey City from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
Most of Jersey City is 5–15 minutes from our 14th Street shop in Hoboken. We typically reach Downtown JC, Hamilton Park, and The Heights non-emergency calls within an hour. Greenville and Bergen-Lafayette are slightly longer (15–25 minutes). 24/7 emergency calls get prioritized.
Do you service the Newport and Paulus Hook condo buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes — we work with several of the Downtown JC condo buildings on per-unit PTAC service programs and common-area HVAC contracts. The newer Newport towers use a mix of PTAC and water-source heat pumps depending on the building era. We service both.
Can you handle steam boilers in old Heights buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes — steam boilers are one of our specialties. Many Heights buildings still run on original steam systems, sometimes 50–80 years old. We service the boiler, the radiators, and the steam piping — or recommend hydronic conversion if it makes economic sense for your specific building.
Do you do single-family in Greenville and Bergen-Lafayette?
 [svg] 
Yes. Single-family south of Liberty State Park is a different stock than the Downtown/Heights work — usually post-war central HVAC, sometimes natural gas, sometimes oil. We do central system replacement, ductwork rework, and heat pump conversions across these neighborhoods.
