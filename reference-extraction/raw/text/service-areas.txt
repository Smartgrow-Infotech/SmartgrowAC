Quick answers
### Common questions about service areas.
Where is your headquarters?
 [svg] 
Our HQ is at 4 14th St #501 in Hoboken, NJ. Address-only office — we’re a service business, no walk-in showroom. Phones answered 24/7 across Hudson County.
Do you charge a travel fee to certain cities?
 [svg] 
No. Pricing is the same across all 10 cities we serve. We don’t add travel fees for Bergen County or distant Hudson County addresses.
Why don’t you serve Bayonne, Secaucus, or Kearny?
 [svg] 
We could, but we focus on the 10 cities where we have the deepest local knowledge and fastest response times. Quality over coverage.
Do you work outside Hudson and Bergen County?
 [svg] 
Occasionally for existing customers who moved. For new commercial accounts, sometimes. Call us — we’ll be honest about whether we can serve you well.
How do you handle emergencies in cities further from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
Phones answered 24/7 regardless of city. Arrival time depends on the situation, traffic, and current scheduling — we’ll tell you straight what to expect rather than make a fake "60-minute" promise.
Is the same team working in all cities?
 [svg] 
Yes — we’re family-owned with the same techs across all service areas. Not a franchise. The person on the phone is local, and the tech who shows up has been with us for years.
