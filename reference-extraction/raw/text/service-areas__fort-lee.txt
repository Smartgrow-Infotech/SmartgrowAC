Fort Lee HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Fort Lee, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 15–20 minutes north via Boulevard East. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for Fort Lee’s diverse housing: Hudson Lights high-rise condos, Main Street and Lemoine Avenue mid-rises, Linwood single-family, and the townhomes near the GW Bridge approach. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re the cross-the-bridge alternative for Fort Lee homeowners who want a Hudson County HVAC company instead of a Bergen County one.
Why Fort Lee works with us
### Fort Lee’s housing is bigger and more diverse than most Hudson County neighbors.
Fort Lee covers more housing variety than most Hudson County towns — full-tower high-rise condos in Hudson Lights and along the GW Bridge approach, mid-rise condos on Main Street and Lemoine Avenue, the older single-family stock in Linwood, and townhomes scattered through the borough. Each housing type has its own HVAC pattern: high-rises run PTAC and WSHP arrays, mid-rises mix systems, single-family runs central HVAC.
We work all of them. Many Fort Lee customers come to us because they prefer the family-owned Hoboken-based feel to the larger Bergen County HVAC contractors. We’re 15–20 minutes away, we know the buildings, and we’ve been doing this in the area for a quarter century. Multi-property condo contracts work the same as our other Hudson County work — predictable per-unit pricing, scheduled execution, real reporting.

Frequently asked in Fort Lee
### Common questions from Fort Lee homeowners.
Do you service Fort Lee high-rises and single-family homes?
 [svg] 
Yes — both ends of Fort Lee’s housing spectrum. Hudson Lights and other modern high-rise condos: per-unit PTAC and WSHP work, common-area HVAC, HOA contracts. Linwood and the older single-family stock: central HVAC replacement, furnace work, ductless add-ons. Same approach, scaled to the property.
How fast can Tanous reach Fort Lee from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
15–25 minutes from our 14th Street shop, depending on Boulevard East traffic and Fort Lee Road access. Hudson Lights and the central Main Street area are typically 15–20 minutes; Linwood and the GW Bridge approach can be 20–25. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 90 minutes.
Do you have relationships with Fort Lee condo HOAs?
 [svg] 
Yes — we work with several of the major Fort Lee condo buildings on per-unit programs and common-area contracts. COI and licensing on file with most major Fort Lee property management companies. New buildings: we send our COI and licensing in advance.
Can you do central HVAC in Linwood single-family homes?
 [svg] 
Yes — Linwood single-family is straightforward central HVAC work for us. Older homes sometimes have oil-burning furnaces ripe for oil-to-gas conversion; newer homes typically already have gas. AC replacement, furnace replacement, ductwork rework all standard. NEIF financing on residential installs.
Do you handle heat pump conversions in Fort Lee?
 [svg] 
Yes — heat pumps are increasingly common in Fort Lee residential, especially as customers consider electrification and NJ utility incentives. Cold-climate heat pumps (Fujitsu Halcyon, Mitsubishi Hyper-Heat) work well in Fort Lee’s winters. We’ll lay out the math vs. gas heat so you see both.
