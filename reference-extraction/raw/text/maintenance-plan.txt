What you get
### What’s included in every visit.
Real maintenance, not a 10-minute drive-by inspection.
[svg] 
### AC tune-up (spring)
Refrigerant level check, coil cleaning, filter replacement, electrical inspection, drain line clearing, capacitor + contactor testing.
[svg] 
### Heating tune-up (fall)
Combustion analysis (gas systems), heat exchanger inspection, ignition + flame sensor check, blower cleaning, thermostat calibration.
[svg] 
### Priority + perks
Front-of-line repair scheduling, 15% off replacement pricing, free diagnostic on repair calls, flexible visit scheduling.
Quick answers
### Common questions about the maintenance plan.
What if my system breaks down between visits?
 [svg] 
Maintenance plan members get priority scheduling on repair calls + free diagnostic visits + 15% off any repair work. Repairs are still billed separately, but at member pricing.
Can I cancel anytime?
 [svg] 
Yes — no contract, no cancellation fee. We bill annually; cancel any time and we won't charge for the next year.
What about multi-system homes (e.g., AC + boiler + 2 zones)?
 [svg] 
Custom pricing — usually $245 + ~$75 per additional system. Call us for a quote based on your actual setup.
Do you do PTAC service contracts for condo buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes, but those are quoted custom based on # of units, building access, and visit frequency. Typical condo PTAC contracts run $40-80 per unit per year. Commercial contracts page →
When do you schedule the visits?
 [svg] 
Spring AC tune-up: March-May. Fall heating tune-up: September-November. We email/text to schedule a few weeks ahead — pick the timeslot that works for you.
What if I'm already due for a tune-up when I sign up?
 [svg] 
We'll schedule your first visit right away if needed. Your annual cycle resets from that visit.
