North Bergen HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served North Bergen, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — about 10 minutes northwest. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for North Bergen’s residential mix: single-family homes in Tonnelle and Greendale, multi-family along Bergenline and Kennedy Boulevard, and the condo developments in Woodcliff. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re built for North Bergen’s mostly-residential character — including the central HVAC work that defines suburban-style streets here.
Why North Bergen works with us
### North Bergen is mostly residential — the HVAC reflects it.
Unlike the high-rise-heavy Hudson County waterfront, North Bergen is mostly suburban-style residential. Single-family homes with central HVAC dominate Tonnelle, Greendale, and Woodcliff. Multi-family walk-ups and small condos run along Bergenline Avenue and Kennedy Boulevard. The HVAC reality is closer to typical New Jersey residential work than the cliff-side condo programs we run in Weehawken or West New York.
We do plenty of straightforward central HVAC replacement, furnace swaps, ductwork rework, and heat pump conversions in North Bergen. The customer base often comes back across the years as different equipment ages out — AC at year 12, furnace at year 18, water heater at year 10. Predictable rhythm. We focus on giving honest replacement-vs-repair numbers, not pushing the most expensive option each visit.

Frequently asked in North Bergen
### Common questions from North Bergen homeowners.
Do you service North Bergen residential single-family systems?
 [svg] 
Yes — single-family central HVAC is most of our North Bergen work. Tonnelle, Greendale, and Woodcliff residential blocks have central AC and furnaces or boilers. We do replacement, ductwork rework, heat pump conversions, and ongoing maintenance contracts.
How fast can Tanous reach North Bergen?
 [svg] 
10–15 minutes from our 14th Street Hoboken shop. The Bergenline corridor portions of North Bergen are slightly faster; the Tonnelle Avenue and Woodcliff areas are slightly longer. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 75 minutes.
Can you do central HVAC replacement in North Bergen?
 [svg] 
Yes — this is bread-and-butter work for us in North Bergen. Most homes have existing ductwork in reasonable shape, so AC and furnace replacements are clean 1–2 day jobs. Manual J load calc included on all installs; no eyeball sizing. NEIF financing available.
Do you do work on Tonnelle Avenue commercial properties?
 [svg] 
Yes — Tonnelle Avenue retail and small office is regular light commercial work for us. Rooftop units, ductless storefront installs, maintenance contracts, after-hours service. Same approach as our Hoboken and JC commercial work.
Do you handle older homes with steam or oil heat?
 [svg] 
Yes — some of the older North Bergen housing stock still runs steam or oil. We service both, and (when it’s time) help homeowners decide between in-kind replacement vs. converting to gas hydronic or heat pump. Both paths have real economic trade-offs we walk you through.
