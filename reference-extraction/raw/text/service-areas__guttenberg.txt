Guttenberg HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Guttenberg, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — just 5 minutes north. Despite being one of the smallest cities in New Jersey by area, Guttenberg packs significant housing density: the Galaxy Towers high-rises, Boulevard East condos, and Bergenline corridor walk-ups make up most of the town. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for all of it. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we cover the high-rise and walk-up realities that define Guttenberg property.
Why Guttenberg works with us
### Guttenberg is small but dense — high-rises plus walk-ups.
Guttenberg is the smallest Hudson County town by area but one of the densest by population. The housing splits between the Boulevard East high-rises (Galaxy Towers and similar) and the Bergenline corridor walk-ups (similar to Union City and West New York stock). PTAC service in the high-rises and shared-boiler / per-unit retrofit work in the walk-ups make up most of our Guttenberg work.
We’re 5 minutes north on Boulevard East, so response time is fast. We know the Galaxy Towers area buildings well and have established HOA relationships in several of the major high-rises. The walk-up multi-family work follows the same playbook as Union City and West New York — per-property contracts, multi-tenant coordination, scheduled execution. Same crew, same response time.

Frequently asked in Guttenberg
### Common questions from Guttenberg residents.
Do you service Guttenberg condos and walk-ups?
 [svg] 
Yes — both. High-rise condos in the Galaxy Towers area: PTAC service, water-source heat pump work, common-area HVAC. Bergenline corridor walk-ups: shared boiler service, per-unit retrofits. Same approach as our Union City and West New York multi-family work.
How fast can Tanous reach Guttenberg?
 [svg] 
About 5 minutes from our 14th Street Hoboken shop. Probably our closest service-area city after Hoboken and the Hoboken-adjacent parts of Jersey City and Weehawken. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 45–60 minutes.
Do you have relationships with the Galaxy Towers area HOAs?
 [svg] 
Yes — we work with several of the major Boulevard East and Galaxy-area condo buildings on per-unit PTAC programs and common-area contracts. Friedrich is the standard PTAC spec in most of these buildings, and we have parts and replacement units stocked.
Can you do work on Bergenline corridor walk-ups?
 [svg] 
Yes — Guttenberg’s Bergenline corridor is similar to Union City’s. 3-family and 4-family walk-up stock, often with shared boilers and individual cooling decisions. Per-property contracts for landlords; per-unit work for owner-occupied units.
Do you handle PTAC emergency repair on weekends and holidays?
 [svg] 
Yes — 24/7 phone answering, dispatch based on emergency severity. Hot guest room in summer or freezing unit in winter is a real emergency to us. Most weekend emergency calls in Guttenberg arrive on-site within 60–75 minutes.
