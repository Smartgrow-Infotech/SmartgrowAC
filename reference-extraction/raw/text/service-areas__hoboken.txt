Hoboken HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has provided HVAC service to Hoboken, NJ for 25 years. Our home base is on 14th Street, and we install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for brownstones, row homes, condos, and high-rises across Downtown Hoboken, Hoboken Heights, Uptown, and the Sinatra Drive waterfront. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses (Contractor #19HC00429300, HVAC #13VH06190200), we’re built for the comfort realities of Hoboken’s pre-war housing stock — including the same-day emergency calls these old buildings often need.
Why Hoboken works with us
### Hoboken is where we started — and where we still live.
Hoboken is where Tanous started in 2001 and where we still keep a shop on 14th Street. We’ve worked on every kind of building this city has — pre-Civil-War brownstones near Stevens Institute, mid-block row homes between Hudson and Park, condos in the Toll Brothers buildings, and walk-ups in Hoboken Heights. The recurring theme: these are buildings that weren’t built for central air, and they don’t have the closet space or wall depth to hide standard ductwork. We solve that more often than anything else — Fujitsu mini-splits in plaster-walled rooms, high-velocity systems where conventional ducts won’t fit, PTAC service in the high-rises along Sinatra Drive.
We also handle the boring-but-critical stuff: emergency boiler service in February, AC swap-outs in heat waves, AprilAire humidifiers for the dry winter air, and the kind of maintenance plan that catches problems before a Hoboken brownstone owner is calling at 6 a.m. for no heat. Three generations of customers across the same families — that’s the result of doing this in one neighborhood for a quarter century.

Frequently asked in Hoboken
### Common questions from Hoboken homeowners.
Do you service Hoboken brownstones and row homes?
 [svg] 
Yes — they’re our specialty. We’ve installed Fujitsu mini-splits in pre-war brownstones across Hoboken Heights, Downtown, and Uptown. We work around plaster walls, original moldings, and tight closet space. Line sets typically route through closets or behind crown moldings — not through demolished plaster.
How fast can Tanous reach my Hoboken address?
 [svg] 
Our shop is on 14th Street in Hoboken. We typically reach Hoboken non-emergency calls within 60 minutes during business hours, and 24/7 emergency calls within 60 minutes whenever we’re dispatched. Hoboken is literally our backyard.
Can you install central AC in a Hoboken building without ductwork?
 [svg] 
Almost always. We use Fujitsu ductless mini-splits, occasionally high-velocity small-duct systems, or a combination of both. We’ve done hundreds of these installs in Hoboken brownstones — usually the conversation is not “can it be done” but “which configuration fits this building best.”
Do you handle Hoboken city permits?
 [svg] 
Yes. We pull all required Hoboken mechanical, electrical, and gas permits, schedule the inspections, and handle the paperwork. You don’t deal with city hall. For condo buildings, we also coordinate with HOA boards for the approvals they require.
Do you service Hoboken’s high-rises along Sinatra Drive?
 [svg] 
Yes. Most Hoboken high-rises use PTAC units (Friedrich is the dominant brand specced in waterfront buildings) — PTAC service and replacement is one of our specialties. We work with most of the major Hoboken condo buildings on per-unit replacement programs.
