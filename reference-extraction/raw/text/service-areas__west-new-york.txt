West New York HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served West New York, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 10 minutes north along Boulevard East. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for West New York’s mix of housing: Boulevard East and Park Avenue condos and high-rises, Bergenline corridor walk-ups, the Galaxy Towers area, and the older multi-family stock between Park and Bergenline. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re built for both the per-unit condo realities and the multi-family-walk-up realities that define West New York property.
Why West New York works with us
### West New York combines high-rise condos with dense walk-up streets.
West New York has two distinct housing characters separated by altitude. Boulevard East and Park Avenue host the high-rise condos and luxury rentals with NYC-skyline views — PTAC and water-source heat pump territory, big per-unit replacement programs. Down the hill on Bergenline and the side streets, the housing is dense 3-family and 4-family walk-ups, similar to Union City — shared boilers, multi-tenant comfort balance, per-unit PTAC retrofits.
We work both. The high-rise condos along Boulevard East (Galaxy Towers area especially) have specific PTAC requirements that we’ve standardized on Friedrich units — reliable, locally-stocked parts, predictable per-unit pricing for HOA programs. The walk-up properties down the hill follow similar economics to Union City multi-family work — per-property contracts, per-unit invoicing where useful, scheduled to minimize tenant disruption.

Frequently asked in West New York
### Common questions from West New York property owners & residents.
Do you service West New York condos and walk-ups?
 [svg] 
Yes — both. High-rise condos along Boulevard East and Park Avenue: PTAC service and replacement programs, water-source heat pump work where applicable. Walk-up multi-family on Bergenline and the side streets: shared boiler service, per-unit retrofits, multi-property contracts for landlords. We work both ends of the West New York housing spectrum.
How fast can Tanous reach West New York?
 [svg] 
About 10 minutes from our 14th Street Hoboken shop. Boulevard East and Park Avenue condos are slightly faster; Bergenline corridor is similar. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 60–75 minutes — we prioritize based on the call situation.
Do you handle PTAC programs for HOA boards?
 [svg] 
Yes — condo HOA work is a major part of our business. We provide written proposals, per-unit pricing breakdowns, COIs for your building, scheduled execution, and per-unit reports for the HOA board. Friedrich is our default PTAC brand — reliable, locally-stocked, predictable warranty.
Can you install ductless in a West New York walk-up unit?
 [svg] 
Yes. For owner-occupied units or with landlord approval, Fujitsu ductless retrofits work well in West New York walk-ups. Line sets typically route through closets or up exterior walls in covers. We coordinate with adjacent unit owners if exterior work affects them.
Do you do multi-property contracts for West New York landlords?
 [svg] 
Yes — if you own multiple buildings across West New York, Union City, North Bergen, etc., one master contract covers all of them. Per-property service plans, consolidated invoicing or per-property as your accounting needs.
