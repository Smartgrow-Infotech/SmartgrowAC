What we hear most
### Common IAQ issues we solve.
If any of these sound familiar, we’ve got the right whole-home solution.
[svg] 
### Dry winter air, static, cracking
Hudson County winters drop indoor humidity below 20%. Causes static shock, cracked wood floors, and dry skin/sinuses. Solution: Whole-home humidifier (AprilAire).
[svg] 
### Allergies acting up at home
Pollen, dust mites, pet dander, and outdoor pollution finding their way inside. Standard HVAC filters catch maybe 30%. Solution: HEPA air purifier (whole-home).
[svg] 
### Musty basement smell in summer
Summer humidity climbs above 60% in older Hoboken/JC basements. Leads to mold, mildew, and that distinctive musty smell. Solution: Whole-home dehumidifier.
[svg] 
### Stuffy air, no fresh feel
Modern tight condos trap CO₂, cooking smells, and humidity. Opening windows wastes heat/AC. Solution: ERV/HRV ventilation — fresh air with energy recovery.
[svg] 
### Pet dander & dust everywhere
Pets shed constantly, and old buildings hold dust no matter how often you clean. Solution: Multi-stage filtration — HEPA + electronic air cleaner combo.
[svg] 
### Frequent colds, illness in family
Airborne viruses and bacteria circulate through ductwork. UV-C light kills them in the air handler. Solution: UV-C purifier on the HVAC.

Quick answers
### Common questions about indoor air quality.
What's the difference between an air purifier and a humidifier?
 [svg] 
Air purifiers filter the air — removing dust, allergens, mold spores, viruses. Humidifiers add moisture to dry air. Different problems, different solutions. Many Hudson County homes need both (winter especially — dry AND dusty).
Do I need an air purifier if I have a good HVAC filter?
 [svg] 
Standard HVAC filters (MERV 8-11) catch about 30-60% of airborne particles. Whole-home HEPA filtration or UV-C systems catch 99%+. If anyone in your family has allergies, asthma, or respiratory issues, an upgraded IAQ system makes a real difference.
What is an ERV and why does my condo need one?
 [svg] 
An Energy Recovery Ventilator (ERV) brings fresh outside air in and exhausts stale inside air — without losing your heating/cooling. Modern tight condos and new construction trap CO₂, cooking smells, and humidity. ERVs solve that without window-opening waste. New NJ construction often requires them.
How often do I change humidifier pads or air purifier filters?
 [svg] 
Humidifier pads: once a year (start of heating season). HEPA air purifier filters: every 6-12 months depending on usage and home dust levels. UV-C bulbs: every 1-2 years. Our Combo Maintenance Plan ($245/yr) includes IAQ filter checks.
Will a whole-home dehumidifier raise my electric bill?
 [svg] 
Some — usually $5-15/month during humid summer months. But it lets your AC run less aggressively (dry air feels cooler), so net bill impact is often a wash. And it prevents the mold/musty smells that cost much more to remediate.
AprilAire or Honeywell — which do you recommend?
 [svg] 
Both are excellent. AprilAire is our default for humidifiers (their bypass and steam units are industry-standard). Honeywell shines for electronic air cleaners and smart thermostats. We’ll recommend based on your specific HVAC setup, not a brand-loyalty pitch.
