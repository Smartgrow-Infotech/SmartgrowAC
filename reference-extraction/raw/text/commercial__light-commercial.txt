What we do
### HVAC for the in-between commercial spaces.
Most commercial HVAC vendors specialize at one extreme or the other: residential split systems on one end, massive corporate HVAC on the other. Light commercial — the offices, retail spaces, and small-business buildings that make up most of Hoboken/JC’s commercial real estate — falls in the gap.
We work it. Rooftop units on retail buildings, mini-splits in renovated storefronts, central HVAC in mid-size offices, dedicated server-room cooling. After-hours service so your business stays open. Predictable per-visit pricing, no surprise charges.
[svg] 
Why light commercial works with us
### What sets us apart for offices and retail.
Light commercial businesses don’t have a facilities department — you need an HVAC partner who acts like one.
[svg] 
### After-hours service
Office HVAC work happens evenings or weekends. Retail rooftop work happens overnight. We default to your operating hours, not ours — minimal disruption.
[svg] 
### Predictable per-visit pricing
Diagnostic visit fees and per-hour labor disclosed up front. No surprise charges. Repeat clients get to know exactly what each visit costs before we arrive.
[svg] 
### NJ licensed + insured
Commercial work requires proper licensing, COI for landlords, and (for some buildings) certified personnel. We have all of it. New buildings: we send our COI in advance of the first visit.

How we engage with light commercial
### From first visit to ongoing service relationship.
Most light commercial engagements start with a repair call and grow into a maintenance contract once the client sees how we work.
01
### First visit & assessment
We respond to your first call (usually a repair or replacement need), diagnose, quote, and execute. While we’re there, we document the rest of your equipment — useful for capex planning later.
02
### Quote & scheduling
Quote written before any work. Scheduling options including after-hours. You approve in writing before we proceed.
03
### Execution + walkthrough
Work performed cleanly, equipment tested, walkthrough so you know what was done. Photos and written summary in your file.
04
### Optional service contract
If repeat work seems likely, we’ll quote a maintenance contract that locks in pricing and includes scheduled visits. No pressure — some clients prefer T&M only. Both work.
What affects pricing
Light commercial HVAC pricing depends on the same factors as any commercial work, sized to your scale.
01
### Equipment type & size
Rooftop unit work has different costs than ductless or central systems. We’ll quote based on what’s actually installed — not generic per-sqft estimates.
02
### Work-hour window
Daytime work: standard rates. After-hours / weekend: modest premium, quoted up front. Most clients find after-hours saves more in lost business hours than the premium costs.
03
### Maintenance vs. ad-hoc
Annual contracts get better per-visit pricing and priority response. Some businesses prefer ad-hoc — we’re fine with both.

Quick answers
### Common questions from light commercial clients.
Do you do single-tenant office buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes — offices from 1,000 sqft up to 30,000 sqft are squarely in our wheelhouse. Single-tenant or multi-tenant, owner-occupied or leased. We handle the work, you handle the business.
What about server-room or IDF cooling?
 [svg] 
Yes — small-office server rooms typically need dedicated cooling (a mini-split or small rooftop unit). We size and install these to handle the 24/7 load that office HVAC isn’t designed for.
Medical or dental offices?
 [svg] 
Yes — medical/dental offices have specific HVAC requirements (filtration, isolation rooms in some cases, dedicated equipment cooling). We work within your existing infection-control protocols and HIPAA expectations.
How fast can you respond to a no-cool emergency in summer?
 [svg] 
Same-day on most calls if you reach us in the morning. Maintenance contract clients get priority dispatch. After-hours emergency response available at modest premium.
Can you handle a building with mixed uses (retail downstairs, residential upstairs)?
 [svg] 
Yes — mixed-use is common in Hoboken. We handle the commercial HVAC and can also handle residential systems above. Single point of contact for the whole building.
Do I need a long-term contract for repair work?
 [svg] 
No — we do plenty of one-off repair work on a T&M basis. Many clients eventually move to a maintenance contract once they see the value, but there’s no pressure either way.
