Why annual maintenance
### What yearly AC tune-ups actually do for you.
Maintenance isn’t a polite formality — it’s the cheapest way to keep cooling reliable through Hudson County’s humid summers.
[svg] 
### Keeps your warranty valid
Almost every manufacturer (Fujitsu, Carrier, Trane, etc.) requires documented annual service to honor the parts warranty. Skip it and you’re on your own when a compressor fails.
[svg] 
### Catches refrigerant leaks early
A slow leak quietly kills efficiency and damages the compressor. Catching it during a tune-up is a $200 fix; catching it after the compressor fails is $2,500+.
[svg] 
### Prevents emergency calls
Most July emergency calls trace back to issues that were visible the previous spring — dirty coils, weak capacitors, loose wiring. We catch them before they break.
[svg] 
### Restores 15–30% efficiency
Dirty coils, clogged filters, and low refrigerant force the system to work harder. A tune-up usually restores measurable efficiency — visible in your utility bill.
[svg] 
### Extends system life
Well-maintained AC commonly lasts 15–18 years. Neglected systems often fail by year 10. Maintenance pays for itself many times over.
[svg] 
### Hygiene & air quality
Coil mold, drain biofilm, and filter dust all show up in your indoor air. A proper clean leaves the system fresh going into peak season.
How a maintenance visit works
### What we actually do on a tune-up.
No clipboard checklist theater. Real cleaning, real measurements, real findings.
01
### Easy scheduling
We hold seasonal slots so you don’t end up booked into a busy week. Pick spring AC, fall heating, or our Combo Plan and we’ll remind you.
02
### Full system tune-up
Multi-point check of mechanical, electrical, refrigerant/combustion, and airflow. Cleaning, calibration, and safety inspection — not a quick once-over.
03
### Honest report
Written findings: what we cleaned, what’s wearing, what to watch next year. No fake ‘repairs recommended’ lists.
04
### Easy renewal
We’ll reach out next season. Same tech, same paperwork. You don’t have to remember a thing.
What affects the price
Our standard tune-up is flat-priced — but a few things change the math.
01
### System count & size
One central AC is single-pricing. A 4-zone ductless or a brownstone with both central + window units takes longer and costs more.
02
### Single vs. combo
Combo Plan ($245) covers both AC and heating tune-ups for the year — cheaper than booking each separately.
03
### Commercial / multi-unit
Property managers and HOAs get a per-unit contract price below the residential rate. We’ll quote you per the building.
Flat residential pricing on our standard plans. For multi-unit buildings, {LINK:tel:2012455151}call 201-245-5151 for a per-property quote.

Quick answers
### Common questions about AC maintenance.
What’s actually included in a tune-up?
 [svg] 
Refrigerant level check, coil cleaning (evaporator + condenser), drain line clear, electrical connection tightening, capacitor & contactor test, thermostat calibration, blower motor & filter check, airflow measurement, and a written findings report. About 60–90 minutes per system.
Single visit vs. Combo Plan — which is right?
 [svg] 
If you have AC and heating, the Combo Plan ($245) is almost always the better deal — covers both seasons and beats two single visits ($185 + $185 = $370). Single $185 makes sense if you only have one season’s system.
When should I schedule?
 [svg] 
Spring AC tune-ups go best in April–May, before peak demand. Once it’s 85°F+ in Hoboken, the schedule fills fast. Booking ahead also means you pick the time, not us.
My building has 4 units. Can you do them all at once?
 [svg] 
Yes — we handle multi-unit condo and rental buildings every week. We quote per-unit, schedule in one visit window, and provide a single invoice or per-unit reports for property managers.
Can I skip a year?
 [svg] 
You can — but the warranty clock keeps running. Most manufacturer warranties require documented annual service to honor parts coverage. Skip a year and a $1,200 compressor claim could get denied.
Does maintenance cover ductless mini-splits?
 [svg] 
Yes. Ductless heads have washable filters, indoor coils, and condensate lines that need annual attention. We service Fujitsu, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Daikin, and others.
