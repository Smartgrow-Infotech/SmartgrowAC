Union City HVAC services
Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Union City, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 5 minutes east. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for Union City’s dense housing stock: 3-family and 4-family walk-ups along Bergenline Avenue, row homes on The Hill, condos in the 32nd Street corridor, and the older multi-family stock around Kennedy Boulevard. With 230+ five-star reviews and 25 years of multi-family-building experience, we’re the local choice for the per-unit HVAC realities that define Union City property.
Why Union City works with us
### Union City’s walk-ups and 3-families need specialized HVAC strategy.
Union City has the densest housing stock in Hudson County — 3-family and 4-family walk-ups are the dominant building type, often built between 1900 and 1940 with shared mechanical rooms, single boilers serving multiple units, and zero ductwork. The HVAC realities here are different from Hoboken brownstones or JC condos: shared boilers need to be designed for multi-tenant comfort balance, per-unit PTAC retrofits need landlord-tenant approval, and ductless installations need careful placement to avoid disrupting adjacent units.
We’ve worked with most of the active Union City property managers and small landlords. We know which 3-family configurations call for per-unit Fujitsu ductless vs. shared central systems, which boiler rooms can be upgraded vs. need full replacement, and which Bergenline corridor walk-ups have permit-friendly access for the work. The economics matter as much as the engineering — we don’t recommend $20,000 jobs on buildings where it can’t come back in rent value.

Frequently asked in Union City
### Common questions from Union City property owners & residents.
Do you service Union City multi-family walk-ups?
 [svg] 
Yes — this is a big part of our weekly work. 3-family and 4-family walk-ups along Bergenline, on The Hill, and around 32nd Street are dominant Union City housing. We do per-unit PTAC programs, shared boiler service, and ductless retrofits for owner-occupied units. Most projects get scheduled to minimize tenant disruption.
How fast can Tanous reach Union City from Hoboken?
 [svg] 
Union City is 5–10 minutes from our 14th Street shop. Bergenline corridor is the closest; the 32nd Street area is slightly further. 24/7 emergency calls typically arrive within 60 minutes — useful for the no-heat calls that hit shared-boiler buildings in January.
Can you handle landlord-tenant approvals for HVAC work?
 [svg] 
Yes — we have the COIs, license documentation, and tenant-notification process most NJ landlords need. We’ve worked with most of the major Union City property managers and small landlords on multi-property contracts. Per-unit invoicing or consolidated billing — whatever your accounting needs.
Do you do shared-boiler service in old multi-family buildings?
 [svg] 
Yes. Many Union City multi-family buildings still have one boiler serving 3–4 units. We service them, repair them, and (when it’s time) help owners decide between in-kind replacement vs. converting to per-unit heating. Both paths have economic trade-offs we walk you through.
Can you install ductless in a single Union City row home or condo?
 [svg] 
Yes — for owner-occupied row homes and condos, Fujitsu ductless is our default for adding cooling to buildings without central HVAC. Most installs in 1–2 days. Permits handled.
