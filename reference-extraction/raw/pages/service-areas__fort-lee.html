Fort Lee HVAC services

Tanous Heating & Air Conditioning has served Fort Lee, NJ for 25 years from our Hoboken base — 15–20 minutes north via Boulevard East. We install and service air conditioning, heating, ductless mini-splits, PTACs, water heaters, and indoor air quality systems for Fort Lee’s diverse housing: Hudson Lights high-rise condos, Main Street and Lemoine Avenue mid-rises, Linwood single-family, and the townhomes near the GW Bridge approach. With 230+ five-star reviews and two NJ master licenses, we’re the cross-the-bridge alternative for Fort Lee homeowners who want a Hudson County HVAC company instead of a Bergen County one.

Why Fort Lee works with us

Fort Lee’s housing is bigger and more diverse than most Hudson County neighbors.

Fort Lee covers more housing variety than most Hudson County towns — full-tower high-rise condos in Hudson Lights and along the GW Bridge approach, mid-rise condos on Main Street and Lemoine Avenue, the older single-family stock in Linwood, and townhomes scattered through the borough. Each housing type has its own HVAC pattern: high-rises run PTAC and WSHP arrays, mid-rises mix systems, single-family runs central HVAC.

We work all of them. Many Fort Lee customers come to us because they prefer the family-owned Hoboken-based feel to the larger Bergen County HVAC contractors. We’re 15–20 minutes away, we know the buildings, and we’ve been doing this in the area for a quarter century. Multi-property condo contracts work the same as our other Hudson County work — predictable per-unit pricing, scheduled execution, real reporting.
