       effect of cooling, and modern oversized systems short-cycle before they
       dehumidify properly. The result is cool, clammy air and mold risk. You
       need dehumidification, not filtration.

     Denver, Chicago, Philadelphia, Dallas — winter dryness. Heated air drops

       need a humidifier.

       your blower.

     New York, and any tight modern build — ventilation. Well-sealed buildings
       trap CO₂ and VOCs. You need mechanical fresh air with energy recovery.

     four, and anyone selling you all four hasn't measured anything."

REPRESENTATIVE ANSWERS (voice reference — write the rest to match)

  Q: Why won't you quote a repair price over the phone?
  A: Because we'd be guessing, and guesses get revised upward once the tech is
     on site. Two systems with identical symptoms routinely need different
     repairs. What we will tell you on the phone is the diagnostic fee, whether
     it credits toward the repair, and roughly what a repair of that type
     typically runs. Then you get a real number in writing before any work.

  Q: Will you push me toward replacement?
  A: No. If a repair is the right call we'll say so, including on older
     equipment. You'll get both numbers and the reasoning. The pattern to watch
     for from any contractor is a modest repair quoted alongside a replacement
     with no middle option — that's a sales script, not a diagnosis.

  Q: Do you actually employ your technicians, or subcontract?
  A: We employ them. Every technician on a SmartgrowInfotech job is on our
     payroll, holds the license their state requires, and works to the same
     documented process. We use licensed trade partners for adjacent scopes —
     electrical service upgrades, roofing around a curb — and we tell you when
     we do.

  Q: What if my location is outside your ten metros?
  A: We'll tell you. If you're just outside a service radius we can often still
     help. If you're genuinely far outside it, we'll say so rather than
     accepting the job and subcontracting it. For multi-site operators we'll
     map which of your locations we can serve well before you sign anything.
